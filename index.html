Disposal Policy

What can't go in the dumpster

For safety and to keep your project on schedule, the following are prohibited in any dumpster or hauling load. If you're unsure about an item, call us before loading.

  • Hazardous materials
  • Asbestos
  • Liquids, paint & solvents
  • Medical waste
  • Chemicals
  • Explosives or fireworks
  • Batteries
  • Sealed containers
  • Tires
  • Fuel, oil or antifreeze
  • Refrigerant appliances*
  • Hot ashes
  • Propane tanks
  • Radioactive material
Prior approval required: Concrete · Dirt · Brick · Rock · Asphalt · Shingles. Heavy materials must be disclosed and must not be mixed with general debris — see Heavy Materials pricing.

*Refrigerators/freezers can be hauled with advance notice — a specialty surcharge applies.
